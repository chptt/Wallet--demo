Capabilities

Built for the serious Web3 user.

Everything you need to interact with Ethereum — nothing bloated, nothing unnecessary.

🔐

Self-Custodial Keys

Your private keys never leave your device. AES-256-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation (210,000 iterations) protects your vault.

⚡

Fast Transactions

Real-time gas estimation with 20% safety buffer. Review fees before confirming. Signed locally, broadcast instantly.

🌐

dApp Provider

Injects a standard window.ethereum provider. Works with Uniswap, OpenSea, and any EIP-1193 compatible dApp.

🔀

Network Switching

Switch between Mainnet, Sepolia, and local Anvil dev nodes in one click. The active network persists for dApp signing.

🌱

BIP-39 Recovery

Standard 12-word seed phrase (BIP-44 derivation path). Import your existing wallet from any compatible device at any time.

✍️

Message Signing

Sign arbitrary messages with EIP-191 prefix via personal_sign. Approval popup shows the decoded message before signing.

Security

Your Keys.
Your Control.

Every private key is encrypted before it ever touches storage. We never see your password, your seed phrase, or your private key.

The chameleon doesn't trust blindly — and neither does your wallet.

🛡️
AES-256-GCM Encryption

Authenticated encryption with 256-bit keys. Detects tampering — a wrong password fails loudly, never silently.

🔑
PBKDF2 Key Derivation

210,000 SHA-256 iterations transform your password into a cryptographic key. Brute-force resistant by design.

🏠
Local Storage Only

Encrypted vault stored in chrome.storage.local — sandboxed to this extension, inaccessible to other sites or extensions.

👁️
Approval-Gated Signing

Every dApp transaction and message signature requires explicit user approval in a separate popup before signing occurs.

🦎
Guardian of Your Keys
Zero knowledge. Zero trust. Full control.
