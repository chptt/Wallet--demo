Why ChameleonWallet

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

A self-custodial wallet built for security, simplicity, and full compatibility with the Ethereum ecosystem.

🔐

Self-Custodial

Your keys, your crypto. Private keys are encrypted locally with AES-256-GCM and never leave your device.

⚡

Instant Transactions

Send ETH in seconds with real-time gas estimation. Review fees before you confirm — no surprises.

🌐

dApp Ready

Connects to any dApp via the standard window.ethereum provider. Works with Uniswap, OpenSea, and more.

🌍

Multi-Network

Switch between Ethereum Mainnet, Sepolia testnet, and local Anvil dev nodes in one click.

🔒

Password Protected

Wallet locks automatically. PBKDF2 key derivation with 210,000 iterations protects against brute force.

🌱

BIP-39 Recovery

Standard 12-word seed phrase. Import your wallet on any device at any time — you're always in control.

Get Started

Up and running in 60 seconds

1

Install the Extension

Add ChameleonWallet to Chrome from the Web Store — or load it unpacked from the source.

2

Create Your Wallet

Set a password and write down your 12-word seed phrase. That's your backup — keep it safe.

3

Start Exploring Web3

Connect to any dApp, send ETH, sign messages, and manage your assets on any EVM network.
